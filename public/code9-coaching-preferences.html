Your GDPR Rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of all data we hold about you
  • Correct: Update or fix any inaccurate information
  • Delete: Request deletion of your account and data (with limited exceptions)
  • Withdraw: Opt-out of any communication or data use at any time
  • Portability: Receive your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@code9.fit
