Peachy rules

How to get the shape — not just the sweat. These rules turn the plan’s three phases into results.

Feel the gluteMind-muscle connection first: slow reps and a 1–2 second squeeze at the top of every glute exercise.
RPE 6–7 to startWeeks 1–3 end every set with 3–4 good reps left. Don’t chase failure yet — build clean movement.
Heavy where it countsHip thrusts, RDLs and squats get heavier from week 4. Arms and shoulders progress slowly, in the 10–15 range.
Burn-it setsWeeks 7–9: on the last set of hip thrusts and abductions, drop the weight by ~30% and keep going.
1.5 repsOne full rep plus one half rep counts as one. Brutal on the shoulders — in a good way.
S/S = supersetDo the two exercises back to back, then rest. Saves time, adds pump.
Toned = muscle + leanThe “toned” look is built muscle with less fat over it — so protein and nutrition matter as much as training.
Log your hip thrustIt’s your headline lift. Beat it by a rep or 2.5 kg every week or two.

Before you start

  • Check with your doctor before starting if you have a heart or lung condition, high blood pressure, diabetes, joint problems or are pregnant — or if it’s been a long time since you exercised.
  • Use a thick pad under the bar for hip thrusts, and set up the bench so it can’t slide.
  • Deficit lunges: a 5–10 cm plate under the front foot is enough — more isn’t better.
  • Stop and seek advice if you feel chest pain, dizziness, unusual breathlessness or sharp joint pain.
