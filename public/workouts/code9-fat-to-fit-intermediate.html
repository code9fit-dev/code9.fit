Before you start

  • Check with your doctor first if you have a heart or lung condition, high blood pressure, diabetes or joint problems, or are pregnant.
  • New to box jumps, sprints or barbell lifts? Get them coached before you go heavy or fast.
  • Stop and seek advice if you feel chest pain, dizziness, unusual breathlessness or sharp joint pain.
