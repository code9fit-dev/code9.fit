How hard should it feel?

Every phase has a target effort on this 1–10 scale. Use the same scale to rate each session in the tracker — it's how your coach knows when to push or pull back.

1–2Very easyBarely working
3–4EasyCould keep going for a long time
5–6ModerateBreathing harder · 4+ good reps left
7–8HardShort sentences only · 2–3 reps left
9Very hard1 good rep left
10MaximalNothing left

Before you start

  • Check with your doctor before starting if you have a heart or lung condition, high blood pressure, diabetes, joint problems or are pregnant — or if it's been a long time since you exercised.
  • Stop and seek advice if you feel chest pain, dizziness, unusual breathlessness or sharp joint pain.
