The Daily
Wellness Protocol

Holistic wellness isn't something you do once — it's something you practise every day. This protocol focuses not just on physical health, but also on your mental, emotional and social well-being, so that reaching your fitness goal goes hand in hand with looking after the whole person.

Use the 9-week roadmap as your guideline: three phases that build the protocol up step by step, until by week 9 it's simply how you live — and you've reached a new level of yourself.

Every day counts. Nine weeks to a new level.
